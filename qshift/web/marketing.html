Questions we answer directly
Does Qshift send quotations autonomously?

No. The proposed scope and price remain a draft until an authorized human approves the exact version.

Can I upload a PDF or photo quotation?

Not in the current supported release. CSV/XLSX imports are the safe supported starting point.

Is the ₹999 plan live?

It is a founding-price hypothesis for pilot discussions, not an activated billing system.

Can I use live customer data today?

Not through this local release. Confidential data requires the production identity, retention and pilot gates to be passed first.
