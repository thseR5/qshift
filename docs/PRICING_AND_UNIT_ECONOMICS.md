# Pricing and unit economics (proposal)

The earlier ₹999/month figure is a hypothesis, not validated revenue. A proposed founding pilot
could include one firm, three users and a capped revision allowance, with onboarding assisted by
the founder. Do not activate billing from this repository.

For each firm, measure: compute hours, PostgreSQL, S3/PDF storage, queue requests, CloudWatch,
model tokens, Meta conversation/template charges, support minutes and onboarding time. Contribution
margin = collected subscription + overage − variable infrastructure − messaging − model − support.

Use the AWS Pricing Calculator after the account region and model are known. Promotional credits
are temporary and must not be treated as a permanent margin assumption.
