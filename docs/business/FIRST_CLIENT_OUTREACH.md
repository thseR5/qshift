# First-client outreach

## Short message

Hi, I’m building Qshift for interior-design teams. It helps turn a client’s messy change request into a reviewed quotation revision using your verified rates, with a human approval step before the PDF is issued.

I’m looking for one or two studios to test a founder-assisted pilot on a representative project. I’m not asking for confidential client data initially. Would you be open to a 15-minute walkthrough of how your team handles quotation changes today?

## Follow-up

The pilot is deliberately narrow. We will use one project, one agreed rate-card extract and one or two changes. I’ll show the exact source evidence, price calculation and approval history, and record any correction rather than claiming the AI was right.
