# 15-minute discovery interview

1. When a client changes a material, size or room, where does the request arrive?
2. Who finds the affected quotation line?
3. How long does a straightforward revision take?
4. What is the most common source of rework or pricing error?
5. Which files are the source of truth: Excel, PDF, ERP or something else?
6. How do you verify rates and GST before sending a revision?
7. Who is allowed to approve a client-facing price?
8. What happens when a client changes their mind after a draft?
9. Which languages and voice notes occur in real requests?
10. What would make a reviewed quotation revision useful enough to pay for?
11. What data could be used in a fictional or sanitized pilot?
12. What would make you stop using the workflow?

Record exact words, date, role, evidence type and whether the participant authorized follow-up. Do not invent testimonials or savings.
