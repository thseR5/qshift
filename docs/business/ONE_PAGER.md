# Qshift — quotation changes you can trust

Qshift helps interior-design teams turn messy client change requests into reviewable quotation revisions.

Forward or paste a request. Qshift identifies the proposed scope change, asks for missing details, matches only verified firm rates, shows the itemized difference and keeps an authorized human in control before a PDF is issued.

## What the first pilot covers

- Assisted onboarding of one studio.
- CSV/XLSX rate-card and quotation import with a mapping preview.
- Explicit rate verification by an owner or approver.
- Supported revision examples such as material replacement and line removal.
- Deterministic INR pricing and GST configuration already present in the firm data.
- Human approval, immutable quote versions and a downloadable quotation PDF.
- Founder-assisted support and manual fallback.

## What it does not claim yet

Qshift is not a general ERP, autonomous pricing negotiator, live production WhatsApp SaaS, voice transcription product or OCR engine. The first pilot uses a limited supported catalogue and fictional/demo data until the real-firm release gates are passed.

## Pilot conversation

We want to understand your current workflow, not force you into a new rate-card format. We can start with one project and one representative revision, compare Qshift's proposed diff with your existing process, and record what needs correction.

Contact: use the founder-approved Qshift contact channel. Do not send confidential client documents until a pilot agreement and data-handling review are complete.
