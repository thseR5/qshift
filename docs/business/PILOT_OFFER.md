# Founder-assisted pilot offer

## Proposed founding hypothesis

₹999 per month plus applicable GST for the first ten firms, with one firm, three users and up to 30 approved revisions per month. Additional approved revisions are hypothesized at ₹25 each. Annual prepay is hypothesized at ₹9,990. This is not an activated billing system.

## Included in the pilot

- Founder-assisted setup.
- One agreed project and supported rate-card/quotation sample.
- Mapping preview and manual rate verification.
- Reviewed draft, exact itemized diff and approval history.
- Manual fallback when an input is unsupported.

## Conditions

- The firm remains responsible for final pricing and client communication.
- Qshift does not approve or negotiate prices autonomously.
- Real data requires written consent, a data-handling decision and Gate C approval.
- Unsupported formats and operations are disclosed instead of guessed.
