# Founder support playbook

## Escalate immediately

- Wrong item, room, quantity, rate or tax.
- Cross-firm access or document visibility issue.
- Approval token, stale version or duplicate-delivery uncertainty.
- Lost or corrupted PDF.
- Privacy, deletion or opt-out request.
- Meta callback, consent or delivery failure.

## Safe response

Pause delivery, preserve the approved version, record a sanitized incident reference, tell the user whether the quote is draft or approved, and use manual quotation preparation until the issue is resolved. Never edit an approved version in place.
