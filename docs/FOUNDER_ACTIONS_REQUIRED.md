# Founder actions required

1. In AWS Billing, record region, plan, credit balance, expiry, Free Tier eligibility and the
   maximum monthly exposure you approve. Run `scripts/aws/account_checklist.sh` first.
2. Confirm whether staging may create billable EC2/RDS/S3/SQS/CloudWatch resources. No deployment
   has been performed.
3. In Meta, confirm the dedicated test/business number, webhook permissions, recipient opt-in,
   messaging window/template rules and any AI-provider eligibility decision.
4. Authorize only a fictional-data test-number run first. Do not attach a real client number.
5. Provide a verified owner/approver enrollment through the operator process; chat cannot grant
   itself a privileged role.
6. After Level 1 evidence, decide whether to approve Level 2 staging and a controlled real-firm
   pilot. Pricing and payment collection remain separate commercial decisions.
